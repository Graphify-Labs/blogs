The Graphify Startups Program is open. Pre-Seed and Seed startups with under $1M in revenue over the past 12 months get 50% off Graphify Teams for up to 6 months. Teams is the hosted plan with one shared graph for every engineer and every coding agent on the team. Apply on the [Graphify Startups Program](https://graphify.com/startups) page.

<Video youtube="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UupGPPVYdis" title="The Graphify Startups Program" />

## Why a small team still needs one map of the code

A small team can ship a product quickly and still lose the thread of how the code connects. Each new chat starts by searching the repo again. Search returns files. The graph returns the path between those files.

A [code knowledge graph](https://graphify.com/blog/what-is-a-code-knowledge-graph) is that path: functions, calls, and imports, with a file and a line on each hop. Teams puts one graph on one bill, so the people and the assistants on the team query the same map.

## Who is the Startups Program for?

Pre-Seed and Seed startups with under $1M in revenue over the past 12 months. The form asks for your funding stage and your revenue range, and you confirm that the revenue stays under that limit.

YC companies and open-source projects have their own offers, below. This program is the 50% discount on Teams.

## What does the Teams discount include?

Approved startups get 50% off Teams for up to 6 months. Teams needs at least 2 seats.

For the first 100 teams, Teams is $29 per seat per month billed monthly, or $20 per seat per month billed yearly. After that it is $40 per seat per month, or $28 billed yearly. The startup price is half of whichever of those you choose. Full rates are on [Pricing](https://graphify.com/pricing).

Teams includes:

- Unlimited shared repositories
- Uncapped graph size
- 100 push builds per repo per day
- 700 pull request reviews per day
- 800 formal verification runs per day
- Shared graphs on one central bill
- A dedicated Slack channel

You can try Graphify before you apply. The open-source CLI is free under Apache 2.0 and needs no account. The hosted Free plan is $0 and needs no card. Teams also has a two-week trial, and a card is required to start that trial.

## How do you apply?

Open the [Graphify Startups Program](https://graphify.com/startups) page and select Apply for startups. The form asks for the company name, website, work email, your role, team size, funding stage, and revenue in USD over the past 12 months. We review the startup and reply by email.

## What about YC companies and open source?

YC companies get 6 months of Graphify Teams free, claimed through Bookface. That offer is on [YC companies](https://graphify.com/yc).

Qualified non-commercial projects with an MIT or Apache license can apply for Graphify free. Projects with 1,000 or more GitHub stars are approved automatically. That offer is on [Open source](https://graphify.com/oss).
