Graphify crossed [100,000 GitHub stars](/blog/100000-github-stars) this summer, 117 days after the first commit. As far as we can tell, that is the first time a Y Combinator company has passed 100,000 stars during its own batch.

We did not set out to break a record. We set out to do one thing well: type `/graphify` in your assistant and get a real map of your codebase back in thirty seconds, running on your machine. The stars followed from that, and so did a community of more than 130 contributors and more than 150 releases.

A batch is a strange, compressed few months. You are meant to find the smallest thing that works and get it to the people who need it. For us that happened in public, on GitHub, with every edge of the graph tagged [`EXTRACTED` or `INFERRED`](/glossary/confidence-tags) and nothing leaving your machine. The people who starred the repo, filed issues, and sent pull requests built this alongside us.

The open-source project stays open source. The always-on version, which maps your code, docs, and meetings continuously and is queryable from anywhere, is in early access at [app.graphify.com](https://app.graphify.com).

Thanks for the stars.

Safi and the Graphify team
